Privacy policy

This policy explains what information NimblyScout receives when you visit the site or send us an inquiry, and how we use it.

When you contact us

The inquiry form asks for your name and email address. Adding a message is optional. We use the information you send to respond by email and follow up on your inquiry.

When you browse the site

You can read the guide and download the tracker without an account. This site does not use analytics tags or advertising cookies. A necessary session cookie protects the inquiry form and temporarily retains your entries if a submission fails. Our hosting provider may process technical details such as your IP address, browser type, and requested pages to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

Who handles your inquiry

Our hosting, form delivery, and email providers may process inquiry information so we can receive and reply to your message. We keep correspondence for as long as needed to handle the inquiry and any follow-up, or as required by law.

Application tracker

The tracker is a downloadable CSV file. NimblyScout does not receive the information you enter into your own copy.

Links to other websites

Other websites handle information under their own privacy policies.

Questions and requests

Email contact@nimblyscout.com or use the inquiry form to ask about this policy or request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you sent us.
